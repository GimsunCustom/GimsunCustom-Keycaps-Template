## How to Use the Templates

### 1. Check Your Keyboard Layout

Before choosing a template, identify:

- Keyboard brand and model.
- Layout and total key count.
- Preferred keycap profile.
- Spacebar and modifier sizes.
- Any unusual or additional keys.
- Switch stem type.

Do not rely on the keyboard’s percentage alone. Two keyboards described as the same size may require different key sizes or arrangements.

**ISO layouts are not supported.** If you are unsure about your keyboard, send us its model and a clear photo or layout diagram.

### 2. Choose the Appropriate Template

Select the file that best matches your intended keycap profile and keyboard layout.

Check the document dimensions, key arrangement, and file format before editing.

If no template matches your keyboard, contact us before modifying another file. Do not assume that a template can be adapted to every layout.

### 3. Download the Original File

Download the original template rather than using a screenshot of its preview.

To download the complete repository on GitHub, select **Code → Download ZIP**.

Keep an untouched copy of the original file for reference.

### 4. Open It in Compatible Software

Choose software that supports the downloaded file format.

| Format example | Editing considerations |
| --- | --- |
| AI | Use a compatible vector editor and check that dimensions, fonts, and layers are preserved. |
| SVG | Open in an SVG-compatible vector editor and confirm the document scale. |
| PSD | Use an editor that supports the file’s layers and effects. |
| PDF | Editability depends on how the document was created and the software used. |
| PNG or JPG | These are raster formats and may not contain editable layers. |

*These are general format examples, not a list of files guaranteed to be included in this repository.*

### 5. Add Your Artwork

Place your illustrations, legends, logos, and colors on the relevant keys.

Preserve the template’s proportions and original dimensions. If separate guide and artwork layers are provided, keep them separate while editing.

For artwork extending across several keys, review both the full composition and the placement on each key.

### 6. Review Your Design

Before submitting, check:

- Spelling, symbols, and language-specific characters.
- Key sizes and positions.
- Artwork orientation.
- Image quality.
- Text contrast and readability.
- Coverage of all required keys.
- Any instructions for side artwork.

Save your editable source file and prepare a clear preview image.

### 7. Submit Your Project for Review

Contact us through [GimsunCustom](https://gimsuncustom.com) with your keyboard information, artwork, and project requirements.

We will review the details with you and confirm the design before production.

## Understanding the Template Guides

For our printing templates:

- The inner outline represents the keycap’s top surface.
- The outer outline represents the keycap’s bottom edge.
- Artwork extending toward the sides must account for the template’s intended coverage and bleed.

Keep important text and recognizable details comfortably within the intended top surface unless side placement is deliberate.

Follow the instructions supplied with your specific template. If the required bleed or artwork boundaries are unclear, ask us before submitting.

### Artwork Across Multiple Keys

Each keycap is a separate physical part with its own edges, gaps, and printing requirements.

A continuous illustration may not align seamlessly across the finished keyboard. Bleed and positioning adjustments can affect how the image connects between keys.

For better results:

- Keep faces, logos, and other critical details on a single key where possible.
- Check how keyboard gaps interrupt the composition.
- Review individual key artwork as well as the full layout.
- Confirm any necessary adjustments during the mockup stage.

## Artwork Preparation Guidelines

### Preserve the Original Dimensions

Avoid unintentionally stretching, cropping, or resizing the template.

A design may look correct on screen while no longer matching its intended physical dimensions.

If you are unsure about the document size, ask us to confirm it.

### Use High-Quality Original Artwork

Use original source files whenever possible.

Vector artwork is useful for clean lettering and logos. For raster images, start with a high-quality source at an appropriate size.

Enlarging a low-resolution image does not restore missing detail. Screenshots and compressed previews may not be suitable for production.

### Keep Text Editable Until Final Review

Check every legend carefully, including punctuation and special characters.

Include font information where needed. If you convert text to outlines for submission, keep a separate editable copy for future revisions.

### Prepare Colors Carefully

Our custom printed keycaps use a white base with color applied through the printing process.

Removing a dark background from your artwork does not create an unprinted black keycap. Unprinted areas may reveal the white base.

Printed colors may differ from your screen because of display settings, materials, printing conditions, and lighting.

For color-critical projects, discuss physical samples and suitable color references with us before production. A digital mockup or product photo is not an exact physical color standard.

### Allow for Fine-Detail Limitations

Very small text, thin lines, and intricate details may appear softer after printing.

Review important details at a realistic size rather than judging them only while zoomed in.

We may recommend adjustments during the design review.

### Specify Side Artwork Clearly

If your design includes artwork on the keycap sides, identify what belongs on each surface.

Printing coverage and shine-through options vary by product. Printed artwork and backlit, shine-through legends are not the same feature.

Do not assume that custom text or a transparent area in an uploaded image can be manufactured as a shine-through legend.

## Keyboard Compatibility Checklist

| Item | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Switch stems | Cherry MX or MX-compatible stems |
| Layout | Matches the template and confirmed product specifications |
| ISO layouts | Not supported |
| Key count | Includes every key needed for your keyboard |
| Key sizes | Spacebar, Shift, Enter, Backspace, and bottom-row modifiers |
| Keycap profile | Matches your intended shape and height |
| Row assignments | Correct placement where the profile uses different row shapes |
| Stabilizers | Suitable for the larger keys in your layout |
| Extra keys | Includes required alternate legends or additional modifiers |

Switch stem compatibility alone does not guarantee a complete fit.

For non-standard layouts, contact us before ordering.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How can I design custom keycaps?

You can download a template from this repository and edit it in compatible software, or choose a product from our [custom keycaps collection](https://gimsuncustom.com/collections/custom-pbt-keycaps) and use its available online customization options.

See our [online customization tutorial](https://gimsuncustom.com/blogs/gimsuncustom-keyboard-keycaps/how-to-order-custom-keycaps-gimsuncustom) for instructions.

### Do you support ISO layouts?

No. We do not currently offer ISO layout customization.

Check your keyboard layout before preparing artwork or placing an order.

### Are your keycaps compatible with Cherry MX switches?

Our keycaps fit Cherry MX and MX-compatible switch stems.

You must also confirm the key sizes, layout, stabilizers, and profile required for your keyboard.

### Do I need to submit a separate design file?

Not always. For supported products, the website’s customization tool can generate the design materials.

If the tool does not accommodate your requirements, contact us with your artwork and keyboard details.

### Can I request a layout that is not included here?

Contact us with your keyboard model and a clear layout diagram.

We will review the request and confirm what is possible before you prepare the artwork. ISO layouts are not supported.

### Can I use my own logo or illustrations?

You can submit artwork you own or have permission to reproduce.

We will review its suitability for the selected keycaps and printing method.

### Can my custom artwork shine through with RGB lighting?

Do not assume that printed artwork can be made into a shine-through design.

Shine-through availability and legend options depend on the specific product. Confirm the available options before designing around backlit text or symbols.

### Will the printed colors match my screen exactly?

An exact screen-to-print match cannot be guaranteed.

Display settings, keycap materials, printing conditions, and lighting can affect the appearance. Discuss critical color requirements with us before production.

### Will artwork align perfectly across several keys?

Seamless alignment should not be assumed.

Key gaps, bleed, and individual key positioning can interrupt a continuous image. Keep critical details on individual keys where possible and review the mockup carefully.

### Does downloading a template place an order?

No. Downloading or editing a template does not place an order or reserve production.

Visit [GimsunCustom](https://gimsuncustom.com) to discuss your project and confirm the ordering steps.
